# The Coffee Connection: A Terminal Cashback Story

## Characters
*   **Sarah (The Customer):** A regular at local cafes, loves getting value for her money, tech-savvy enough to use apps but dislikes complicated processes.
*   **Marco (The Business Owner):** Owner of "Bean There," a cozy coffee shop. He uses a vintage cash register and takes mostly cash or direct card payments. He wants to offer loyalty rewards like the big chains but can't afford expensive POS integration.

## Scene 1: The Transaction
**Location:** "Bean There" Coffee Shop
**Time:** Tuesday, 10:00 AM

Sarah orders two cappuccinos and a slice of carrot cake.
**Marco:** "That'll be £12.50, please."
Sarah hands over a £20 note. Marco rings it up on his old till and hands her the change and a paper receipt.

**Marco:** "By the way, we're on MCOM now. You can get cashback on this order. Just scan that QR code on the counter."
Sarah looks at the small acrylic stand next to the sugar jars. It has a QR code and says "Scan for Cashback".

## Scene 2: The Claim (Customer Flow)
**Location:** Sarah's Phone

1.  **Scan:** Sarah opens her camera, scans the QR code.
2.  **Landing:** She is redirected to `mcom.app/claim/BEANTHERE`.
    *   *System Check:* Sarah is already logged into her MCOM account. If not, she would be prompted to login/signup.
3.  **The Interface:** The page displays "Bean There Cashback".
    *   *Input:* It asks for "Proof of Purchase".
4.  **Upload:** Sarah taps "Take Photo", snaps a picture of her paper receipt, and confirms the upload.
5.  **Selection:** The system asks: "How much did you spend?" (Level 1 Flow).
    *   Options displayed:
        *   £1 - £5 (Get £0.50)
        *   £6 - £15 (Get £1.50)
        *   £16 - £30 (Get £3.00)
6.  **Submission:** Sarah selects "£6 - £15". She sees the estimated reward: £1.50. She taps "Submit Claim".
7.  **Feedback:** Screen updates: "Claim Submitted! Waiting for Marco to approve."

## Scene 3: The Approval (Business Owner Flow)
**Location:** Behind the counter / Marco's Tablet

1.  **Notification:** Marco's tablet (open to the MCOM Dashboard) chimes. A notification badge appears on the "Terminal Cashback" tab.
2.  **Dashboard View:** Marco taps the tab. He sees a list of "Pending Claims".
    *   Top item: "Sarah T. - Just now - Claiming £1.50".
3.  **Verification:** Marco taps "Review".
    *   He sees the photo Sarah just uploaded (the receipt for £12.50).
    *   He sees she selected the "£6 - £15" range.
    *   *Mental Check:* The receipt matches the order he just served.
4.  **Action:** Marco taps the green "Approve" button.
5.  **Result:** The claim moves to "History" as "Approved".

## Scene 4: The Reward (Completion)
**Location:** Sarah's Phone

1.  **Notification:** A moment later, Sarah gets a push notification: "Cashback Approved! £1.50 added to your wallet."
2.  **Verification:** She opens her MCOM Dashboard.
    *   In the "My Wallet" section, she sees the new transaction: "+£1.50 from Bean There".
    *   She smiles, "Coffee tastes better when it's cheaper."

## Scene 5: The Dashboard (Review)
**Location:** Marco's Office (Evening)

1.  **Overview:** Marco logs into his Dashboard to check the day's stats.
2.  **Stats:** He sees:
    *   Total Claims Today: 15
    *   Total Cashback Given: £22.50
    *   Pending Claims: 0
3.  **Configuration:** He decides to run a special for the weekend. He goes to "Settings" and adjusts his cashback ranges to be more generous for higher spends, preparing for the weekend rush.
